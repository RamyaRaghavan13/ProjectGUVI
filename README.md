Myntra Business Analysis: Online Reputation & Market Insights
📌 Overview
This project analyzes Myntra's online reputation, customer satisfaction, market trends, and competitive landscape using insights from social media and customer reviews. The goal is to provide data-driven recommendations to enhance Myntra’s market position and customer engagement.

📊 Business Use Cases
✔ Brand Perception – Analyze customer sentiment from reviews and social media mentions.
✔ Customer Satisfaction – Identify key factors influencing customer satisfaction and areas for improvement.
✔ Market Trends – Discover emerging fashion trends and changing consumer preferences.
✔ Competitive Landscape – Benchmark Myntra’s performance against competitors and suggest differentiation strategies.

🔍 Key Findings
Strengths: Extensive product variety, affordability, and frequent sales events.
Challenges: Delivery delays, return policy concerns, and customer service issues.
Opportunities: AI-driven personalization, exclusive brand collaborations, and sustainability initiatives.
📂 Project Structure

📑 Conclusion
This analysis provides actionable insights for Myntra to enhance its brand reputation, improve customer satisfaction, and gain a competitive edge in the online fashion industry.
